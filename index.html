About the Studio

Tumbuktu Studio is a creative photography collective dedicated to capturing the raw beauty of human stories across continents. Founded in 2020, we blend editorial precision with cinematic atmosphere to create visual narratives that transcend the ordinary.

Our work spans fashion editorials, travel documentaries, intimate portraits, and nocturnal cityscapes. Every frame is a deliberate meditation on light, emotion, and the spaces between.
